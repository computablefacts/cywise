HOW TO READ YOUR EXTERNAL ATTACK SURFACE

Your external attack surface includes everything an attacker can see from the Internet. This guide explains how to map it and what to fix first.

CYWISE LAB26.08.20268 MIN
IN THIS ARTICLE 01020304

1. Start with visible assets

Domains, subdomains, IP addresses, ports and public services form your first view. The goal is also to find forgotten or unmanaged assets.

2. Look at context, not only counts

One hundred low-severity findings do not always matter more than one critical exposure. Prioritize by accessibility, asset sensitivity and real exploitability.

3. Look for changes

An attack surface changes with new services, migrations and temporary environments. Continuous monitoring helps detect new exposure quickly.

4. Turn findings into actions

Each finding should lead to a simple decision: fix, close, update, monitor or accept. A short prioritized list helps the IT team move forward.

The useful question is not “how many vulnerabilities do we have?”. It is “what is actually exploitable today?”.
